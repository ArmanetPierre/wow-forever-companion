Blizzard's developers explicitly confirmed this during Episode 3 of the official WoW Forever podcast on 8 October 2026. (source : Warcraft Tavern)

- Per character: Every character wanting to enter Onyxia's Lair must complete the attunement questline independently.

- Equipment: You don't need to equip it; having it in your bags is sufficient.

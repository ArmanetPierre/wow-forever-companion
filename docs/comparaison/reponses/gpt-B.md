Attention : nouvelle précision du 17 novembre

Exemple :

- Joueur 38 contre monstre 25 : XP annulée pour tout le groupe sur ce monstre.

La limite concerne chaque monstre individuellement, pas simplement le niveau du boss du donjon. [WoW Forever]

Le hotfix du 17 novembre n'a pas réduit davantage l'XP des donjons : ses changements concernent notamment certains boss du Temple englouti et de la Cité engloutie. [VICE]

## C12 — Onyxia attunement account-wide? (EN)

No. Onyxia attunement is NOT account-wide in WoW Forever (at launch, 4 November 2026).

Blizzard's developers explicitly confirmed this during Episode 3 of the official WoW Forever podcast on 8 October 2026. [Warcraft Tavern +1]

Here's what that means:
- Per character: Every character wanting to enter Onyxia's Lair must complete the attunement questline independently.
- Required item: Each character needs their own Drakefire Amulet.
- Equipment: You don't need to equip it; having it in your bags is sufficient.
- Alts: Completing the chain on Brannok won't unlock Onyxia for your other characters.
- No shortcut: The Chipped Drakefire Amulet shortcut available in some other Classic versions isn't available in Forever. [conquestcapped.com +1]

For Brannok (Alliance): your questline will start at level 48 with Dragonkin Menace, given by Helendis Riverhorn in the Burning Steppes.

You'll eventually need to complete quests involving: Burning Steppes, Blackrock Depths (BRD), Stormwind, Upper Blackrock Spire (UBRS).

Source: WoW Forever – Onyxia Attunement Guide.

My recommendation: don't worry about it yet at level 25. You've got plenty of time before the raids open on 9 December 2026. 😁
